For fun

A room for wrecking tensegrities

Part of HMR is having fun. Every structure here stands the way the body does: struts held apart by tension. The room fills your window. Drag to move the world, then pick a tool from the bar at the bottom.

Tools
Disasters
Time and surprises

Controls: drag to move the world, drag with Shift or the right mouse button to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and press Escape to leave the room. On a phone, drag with one finger to move, pinch to zoom, and twist with two fingers to turn. What to notice: cut one cable and watch the load move through the whole structure. Slack cables pull tight, and each line changes color as it takes more strain. The toy people and cars follow the same physics: they ride whatever is under them, fall when it goes, and get tossed by quakes, wind, and the tornado. The working human model is on the simulation page.

In short

Part of HMR is having fun. This page is a room full of tensegrities to play with: structures held up by tension and struts together. Cut them, break them, burn them, shake the ground under them, send a storm through them, and then set them right again. Every structure here stands the way the body does, so playing with them is a quick way to feel how tension and compression share a load.
