HMR physics.

The HMR keeps every equation physics has confirmed. What it changes is the framework the equations are read in: space, time and matter are not the bottom layer of reality, Universal Intelligence (UI) is. Read that way, physics' open problems change shape. This page sets them side by side, then makes a serious attempt at the hardest one, gravity.

The hardest problem

Curved spacetime, from the first distance.

On the Science Bridge, the mathematics runs from the first distance to flat spacetime, the spacetime of special relativity. The next step is curved spacetime: gravity. Here is the HMR's attempt. It does not invent new physics. It walks a route physicists already know, and supplies the one piece that route leaves unexplained.

1. Boundaries carry information in proportion to area standard

A black hole's entropy, a measure of the information it holds, is proportional not to its volume but to the area of its boundary, in units of the Planck length squared (Bekenstein, 1973; Hawking, 1975):

S = kB A / (4 ℓP2)

2. Einstein's equations follow from that standard

Ted Jacobson showed that if every small patch of spacetime boundary obeys the law of heat, δQ = T dS, with entropy proportional to area, then Einstein's equations of gravity follow. Gravity becomes an equation of state, like the gas laws, for something deeper (Jacobson, 1995; a related route in Verlinde, 2011):

δQ = T dS  and  S ∝ A  ⟹  Gμν = (8πG / c4) Tμν

What the route does not say is what the area is counting. That is the open piece.

3. What the area counts: units of distance HMR

In the HMR, all of space is built from repetitions of one unit of distance, ℓP. A boundary is crossed by relations, each one unit long. The number of relations that can cross a surface is the number of unit cells on it:

N = A / ℓP2  ⟹  S ∝ N ∝ A

That is exactly the area law step 2 needs. So the HMR supplies what Jacobson's derivation leaves unexplained: the entropy of a boundary is the count of first distances crossing it.

4. Coherence displaces distance HMR

Matter, in the HMR, is coherent structure. When energy, coherent structure, crosses a boundary, it is heat δQ in Jacobson's sense; the change in the number of unit relations crossing the boundary is the change in area. Coherence moving through distance reshapes distance. In Einstein's language, matter tells spacetime how to curve. In the HMR's, coherence tells distance how to be displaced.

5. What is still open open

  • Derive the factor of 1/4 in step 1 from the HMR's counting, rather than borrowing it.
  • Show that the counting gives the right temperature, so that δQ = T dS holds exactly.
  • Find a prediction where this account and standard general relativity differ, and test it.

Until the last item exists, this is a coherent route, not a result.

Why this matters

Gravity is the one force quantum physics cannot express. If gravity is not a force at all but the large-scale bookkeeping of the units of distance, that would explain why: there would be nothing to quantize beyond the units themselves, and the units are what the HMR says came first.
