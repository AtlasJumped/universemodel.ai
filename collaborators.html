The ask

What we are asking for

Above all, engineering help: building the simulation of the human body, and carrying the mathematics and physics past where they stand today. Here are the problems, stated as plainly as we can.

Engineering

Simulate the body, unit by unit

The goal is a physics simulation of the whole body at the finest grain that computing allows. The atom is out of reach, and so, most likely, is the molecule. Something near the cell may be a reasonable target.

We define one unit and call it a cell. It has a tissue type, a 3D shape, a surface with points where it can join or let go of its neighbors, and the properties it needs to pass on force, pressure, and heat. It holds a set amount of material and energy, with a healthy baseline.

We begin with a master unit that can become any type, the way a stem cell can. We fill the shape of a human with master units, then use imaging to turn each one into the tissue found at that place.

Mathematics and engineering

Define the ideal biotensegrity

HMR holds that there is an ideal arrangement of bones and connective tissue (often called fascia). Exactly what it is has to be worked out. Should the bones of the spine stack straight up and down, or not, and why?

The outside shape can start from images of well-built people, and the bones from imaging. Bones could be treated as rigid at first. The aim is to model them in full, because bone also makes blood cells, and the whole system has to be able to be healthy or unhealthy.

Machine learning

Gather the data and give it structure

This needs a very large set of data with a very large number of features: the properties of every tissue, healthy and unhealthy, how tissues act on each other physically, and what happens to their chemistry when the physics changes.

We have begun collecting it (see the library). We need people who know how to use machine learning to gather it properly and build with it.

Engineering

Put the body in the world

Next comes a bank of things in the environment that are suspected of causing disease, each described by its physics. Radio waves, microwaves, and gamma rays, for example, are energy arriving at different frequencies.

We want to place the simulated body in a setting, such as a home beside power lines, see whether and how the tensegrity changes, and follow what comes downstream of that change.

Mathematics and physics

Finish the mathematics and physics

The Math and Physics pages take HMR's account from the first distance toward the known laws. Each open step is marked there. We want mathematicians and physicists to close them, or to show where they cannot be closed.

Machine learning

Train an HMR guide

We want an assistant that knows HMR thoroughly: a combination of friend, family member, doctor, and expert that anyone can talk to. It would take a question through all of HMR's models, the way the Answers page does, and help people with good ideas work out how to carry them through.

We are testing this ourselves first. Building it properly means training and evaluating a language model on HMR, and that needs someone who knows how.

Machine learning

Simulate a society's values

Model the values that guide a country, then many countries. Then simulate what happens, decision by decision, in trade, treaties, and the sharing of resources, if one country adopts HMR's values, or the whole world does.

HMR's hypothesis: if human behavior and values can be modeled well, HMR's value system will come out as the best one for any society to adopt. A model that captures it could also guide a very capable AI agent.

What we bring

A dedicated human effort, a working website, data already collected and stored, and good hardware to run it on.
