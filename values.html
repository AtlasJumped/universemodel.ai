HMR Values

Values that follow from one reality

If everything is one Universal Intelligence (UI) experiencing itself, then how we live is not a matter of taste. The Holistic Model of Reality (HMR) gives a reason for each value, drawn from what reality is and what our role in it is.

Why values need a model of reality

Five questions every value system has to answer

What is reality?
What is my role in it?
What are my goals?
What are the goals of other beings?
How do I weigh my goals against theirs?

A holistic model of reality answers all five from one theory. A partial model, such as a purely material one, answers almost none of them: it describes how matter behaves, but says nothing about purpose, role, or what we owe each other. That has a hard consequence. Anyone who has built a value system on a wrong picture of reality may be living in ways that do not match their own true values. Anyone who has never thought their values through is living by a value system with no reason behind it at all.

That gap is one reason many people take their values from religious texts written long ago. Those texts carry real and lasting wisdom. HMR aims to answer the same questions from a model of reality that also includes everything science has established. Why the material picture is incomplete →

The starting point

If we are all one, life is on one mission

In HMR, every living being is an instance of the same UI, placed in physical reality to experience it. If that is so, life is not a collection of strangers competing for resources. It is a collaboration with an aligned purpose.

Humanity is the tip of the spear: the most capable instance UI has produced so far, able to understand itself, shape its world, and help other life. With that capability comes responsibility to further UI's interests, which means two things above all: acting truthfully, and enabling other life to enjoy physical reality and benefit from it.

Pushing our limits is being true to our nature. In HMR, the Big Bang itself came from UI testing what it is and what it is not, exploring every boundary (Defining Universal Intelligence). UI is still maturing, and we take part in that maturation. That is why we have a stake in the whole universe benefiting from how we act, and why we become better actors the same way UI did: by pushing our own limits, and paying close attention to how doing so makes us feel and how it makes others feel.

If people are, in effect, UI's most capable agents, the most capable beings in the universe that we know of, then what we do with our minds and our bodies matters a great deal. Physical life is also not the whole story: HMR holds that existence continues after physical reality.

The golden rule, as a mission

The first golden rule in HMR is this: UI is maturing, and each of us is part of that maturation. Own your role in it. The familiar golden rule, treating others as you would want to be treated, follows from it, for a reason that goes beyond fair exchange. You are here to help UI experience and grow through this reality, and so is everyone else, so the more we help each other, the more we all thrive. Treating others as you would want to be treated in their place is not only kind; it is accurate. In HMR, others are the same intelligence you are, living in another body.

A compass

With a clear account of what humans are, and how they relate to each other, to other creatures, and to UI, questions that seem to have no answer start to have one.

What is reality→What am I→What is my role→How should I act
Why we relieve suffering

In HMR, suffering is a signal that something in the model is wrong, and life is what can feel that signal. A rock falling into the sun feels nothing and cannot act. A living thing feels its own undoing and acts to change its future. So relieving suffering in ourselves, in other people, and in other creatures is repairing the model. This is the rational basis for empathy in HMR.

Truth

A lie corrupts the model

In HMR, UI is running a truthful reality and seeks truthful signals from the beings in it. A lie, and especially deliberate confusion such as gaslighting, puts a false signal into that system. It hurts the person deceived, it hurts the whole, and it hurts the one who lies, more directly than a materialist view would expect, because that signal returns to the same intelligence the liar is part of.

Belief

What you believe, you become

In HMR, how you think and what you believe really does show up in physical reality. You can reprogram your own mind, just as you can reprogram your connective tissue (often called fascia). The traditions of Asia found the tools long ago: mantras, chants, and repeated affirmations rewrite the mind's software, alongside the work of freeing the body. The mind is a tool for forging values, not only for holding them.

What you repeat, you come to believe, and what you believe, you become. Many people know this idea as manifestation, and it has a long line of proponents: the New Thought writers of the 1800s, Napoleon Hill's Think and Grow Rich (1937), Neville Goddard in the 1940s and 1950s, and Rhonda Byrne's The Secret (2006).

Where the evidence stands: science has measured narrower versions of this, such as the placebo response and the way a firm expectation changes what a person does. It has not confirmed that belief alone changes the world outside the believer. HMR makes the stronger claim, as a hypothesis.

Fairness

Weighing people, and institutions

The same standard could help judge cases between people, and between corporations and people. In HMR there is a real good and a real not good, so any deviation from the good can, in theory, be measured.

The values

Fifteen values derived from HMR

A general set, each traced to a premise of the model. They are not commandments; they are what HMR's account of reality implies.

Be truthful

UI seeks truthful signals; every lie distorts the reality we all share, starting with the liar's own.

Treat others as you would want to be treated in their place

Every being is an instance of the same UI, so harm to another is harm to the whole you belong to. You are not them: imagine yourself in their situation, with their history and needs, and treat them as you would want to be treated there.

Empower others

Our shared purpose is for life to enjoy and benefit from physical reality; what you make possible for others is the measure of contribution.

Cooperate

Life is one collaboration with an aligned mission; competition that harms the whole works against everyone in it.

Care for all life

Animals share the same consciousness in different bodies. Do not kill them unless survival truly requires it.

Responsibility grows with capability

Humanity is UI's most capable instance so far; the more power a person or institution holds, the more it owes the rest of life.

Seek coherence

UI's changes trend toward coherence; align what you think, say, and do, with yourself and with others.

Grow through limits

Physical life is where we push our limits and learn good from not good; failure is part of that learning, not a final verdict.

Choose your beliefs deliberately

Belief shapes physical reality; use repetition, affirmation, and attention to build the values you mean to hold.

Enjoy the experience

UI limited itself to focus on more pleasant thought; enjoying physical reality well, without harming others, is part of the purpose, not a distraction from it.

Stay open, keep testing

The model is incomplete; hold claims in proportion to evidence, keep what holds up, and revise what does not, this list included.

Practice perspective

Perspective is the main factor in happiness, and it is a skill. A person can be happy in a jail cell or miserable in a mansion. Practice it with intention and learn from mistakes, and the mind becomes attuned to finding the good in every situation it can, in the full context of reality.

Appreciate

Every moment spent in appreciation is a moment well spent. Appreciation is how an instance of UI enjoys what UI made, and it costs nothing.

Care for your connective tissue

The body is UI's interface with physical reality, and connective tissue sets its shape. Caring for it daily improves your health, your thinking, and everything you can give. The protocol →

Take part in UI's maturation

UI is still growing up, and you are part of how it does. Push your limits, learn from your mistakes, and help others do the same; that is your role, and it is worth owning.

In short

Most people inherit their values and seldom ask what they rest on. In HMR, values are not a matter of taste. If everything is one Universal Intelligence experiencing itself through every living thing, then how we treat ourselves, each other, and the world follows from what reality is and from our part in it.
