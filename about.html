About

An independent research program.

Universe Model is the research program of Leo Emerson and the home of the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR).

The founder.

Leo Emerson has spent more than five years developing the Holistic Model of Reality: a single account that connects the physics of the universe, the human body and the experience of being alive. Its most concrete and testable part is the body as a tensegrity held together by fascia, and that is where the research starts.

The program is independent. It works with AI research assistants running on a dedicated research workstation, and every conclusion is checked against its original sources.

The Holistic Model of Reality.

The HMR proposes that physical reality emerges from an underlying, non-physical intelligent process, Universal Intelligence (UI), whose changes trend toward greater coherence, and that the human body is the vessel through which each person experiences that reality. It draws on both modern science and older traditions, including Hindu philosophy's account of Brahman and Atman.

These are hypotheses and are presented as such. The health model described on this site is designed to be tested on its own scientific terms, whatever one thinks of the larger view.

Principles.

Evidence first

Every finding is traced to its original source and labeled as established or as hypothesis.

Honest about doubt

Evidence against the model is kept as carefully as evidence for it.

Patient

We would rather be right than early. Nothing is presented as finished before it is tested.

For everyone

The aim is a model of health that people anywhere can understand and benefit from.
