What Universe Model is.

Universe Model presents the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR): a narrative for humanity that explains reality as a whole, and a program to model it in working simulations.

The Holistic Model of Reality.

The HMR proposes that physical reality emerges from Universal Intelligence (UI), a non-physical intelligence that has matured over time toward greater coherence, and that each human is an instance of UI living through a physical body. It draws on modern science and on the world's contemplative traditions, and is not affiliated with any religion.

Its most concrete and testable part is the human body understood as a tensegrity held together by fascia. That is where the work begins: with a new model of health, and a simulation of the ideal human body.

Why it matters.

What we believe reality is shapes how we live, how we care for our bodies and each other, and what we think is possible. A model that joins physics, the body, consciousness and the traditions into one account offers a different and more hopeful picture of what a human being is, and of what a human body can become.

The HMR's claims are hypotheses and are presented as such.
