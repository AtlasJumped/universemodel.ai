What Universe Model is.

Universe Model presents the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR): one connected account of reality, from before the Big Bang to the human body, and a research program to put its most concrete claim to the test by building the human body as a working, physics-based simulation.

1. The problem

The problems humanity faces share a root.

Chronic disease is rising and is rarely reversed. Mental illness is widespread and poorly understood. People are divided over what is true, what is right and what life is for, between neighbors as much as between nations.

These look like separate problems, handled by separate experts: doctors, psychiatrists, scientists, priests, politicians. The HMR's view is that they share a root. After thousands of years of philosophy and centuries of science, humanity still cannot say what reality is. It has no agreed account of what a human being is, or of what a healthy body and a sound mind look like.

Without a shared picture of reality
  • Health has no defined healthy body to measure against.
  • Mental health has no agreed account of what is real to measure minds against.
  • Science describes how reality behaves, not what it is.
  • Values have nothing firm to rest on, so they divide us.

Every field holds a piece. No one holds the picture.

Each discipline works deeper and deeper into its own corner, and the corners never meet. It is like a thousand people each assembling one part of a jigsaw puzzle, none of whom has ever seen the picture on the box.

2. Models of reality

Everyone has a model of reality. Almost no one has a holistic one.

A model of reality is the picture of the world that any living thing acts on: what exists, how it behaves, what matters. Having one is not optional. A blade of grass, an animal and a human each operate on one. Each model has higher or lower fidelity than another, depending on the sensory hardware that gathers it and, possibly, the mental faculties that interpret it. A bat's world is built from echoes, a bee's from ultraviolet light, a human's from five senses and the instruments built to extend them.

Humanity's best models to date are partial. Physics models matter, biology models life, medicine models disease, psychology models the mind, religion models meaning. Each can be divided into ever finer detail, and still miss what only appears when the system is seen as a whole.

A holistic model of reality is different in kind. It joins the parts into one connected account, so that understanding one part gives insight into every other.

Why holistic matters

When a system is understood as a whole, it can be modeled and changed with high fidelity. When even one important variable is unknown, that fidelity can fall to nearly zero, however precisely everything else is measured.

Picture an engineer who knows every part of a bridge except where it is anchored. The calculations can be perfect and the bridge still falls. Humanity's models of reality are in that position: excellent in their parts, with the anchor missing.

Or picture a navigator with flawless charts of every coastline who does not know where the ship is. Every chart is correct, and the voyage is still lost. Detail cannot replace the one fact that ties the details together.

3. The Holistic Model of Reality

One account, from before the Big Bang to the human body.

The HMR proposes that physical reality emerges from Universal Intelligence (UI), a non-physical intelligence that has matured over time toward greater coherence, and that each human is an instance of UI living through a physical body. It draws on modern science and on the world's contemplative traditions, and is not affiliated with any religion.

Its most concrete and testable part is the human body understood as a tensegrity held together by fascia. That is where the work begins: with a new model of health, a model of mental health, and a physics-based simulation of the ideal human body.

A doctor who knew every organ but had never seen a living person could still describe a body in great detail, and still not know what it is for. The HMR is an attempt to supply that missing view: what the whole is, so that the parts make sense.

What the HMR covers

Select a topic for the HMR's short answer. Each is a hypothesis of the HMR.

The origin of physical reality and its laws

Before physical reality, the HMR describes UI as unlimited intelligence without order. It settled on orderly thought, and its first structured thought separated a "me" from a "not me": a distance between two points. From that came spacetime, then matter. The laws of physics are the rules UI holds fixed so that many separate beings can share one stable world. More →

Why mathematics describes nature

Science has never explained why the universe obeys equations. In the HMR the answer is direct: mathematics came first. Geometry, ratio, relationships and π were among the original orderly thoughts from which physical reality was built, so nature is mathematical because it is made of mathematical thought.

What life, death and a human being are

Life is UI placing many instances of itself into one shared world, from grass to animals to people, so that they learn from one another. A human being is UI's most advanced instance in physical reality. Death is a transformation, not an end: the experience of a life is kept and added to UI's understanding, and the transition may differ for every form of life.

The body as a tensegrity, and disease as deviation from its ideal

Bones act as struts and fascia as a continuous web of tension around them, so the body holds its shape the way a tensegrity does. A perfect tensegrity is the reference for full health. Every physical condition is a measurable deviation from it, and the HMR's first test, the densification hypothesis, asks whether denser fascia alone can produce the shapes of real disease.

The mind, sanity and mental health

Sanity can be measured three ways: how true a person's picture of reality is, whether they can achieve what they set out to do, and how they meet other people. Today's institutions use the last two, because the first needs an agreed account of reality. The HMR also links the mind to the body: posture, tension and the brain's maps of the body shape how a person feels and thinks. More →

How the world's traditions and modern science describe the same source

UI is what traditions have called God, Brahman or the Tao. People who studied their own bodies and minds for a lifetime, in yoga, martial arts and meditation, found practices that work on fascia and found a source behind reality. Science measures the same body from outside. The HMR offers one language for both. More →

4. Why it matters

The world a holistic model makes possible.

Many of the deepest tensions between people come from ambiguity: how we should treat animals, what we should eat, how we should treat other people and how we should think of them. Under the HMR, that ambiguity disappears.

People who disagree today about life and death, about what is real and what is not, would move from disagreement to agreement on the nature of reality, possibly for the first time in human history. Nations at war might not agree on everything, but they would agree on that.

In the HMR, every living creature is an instance of the same Universal Intelligence. Suffering anywhere is suffering within the one reality we all share. A child starving on the other side of the world matters as much as a child starving next door, and the suffering of an animal is not invisible because no one has to look it in the eye.

Everyone would have a reason to end their own conflicts, and every country at peace would have a reason to help others end theirs. The purpose of a human life becomes clear: to help minimize the suffering of all creatures.

Health

Mind

Mental health measured against an agreed account of what is real and a defined healthy body, so that suffering is understood instead of feared.

Peace

Conflict loses its deepest fuel when the people on both sides agree on what is real and what matters. Ending conflict becomes in everyone's interest.

Science

A program that keeps everything science has established and gives its open questions, from before the Big Bang to consciousness, a framework to be tested in.

Traditions

The insight of the world's body practices and contemplative traditions, finally readable in the language of physics.

HMR hypothesis

A future that is totally different.

If the universe emerges from Universal Intelligence, then distance is not a fixed feature of reality: it is a rule within that intelligence, and in the HMR's view an illusion, much as Hindu thought describes the world of separation as maya. What is a rule can, in principle, be worked with. The HMR's teleportation theory follows: teleportation should be possible, and with it access to effectively unlimited resources, ending scarcity as the reason people compete.

This is the most speculative part of the HMR, and it is stated as a hypothesis, not a result. It shows how far the consequences reach if the model is right.

Today, few people hold the HMR. Imagine billions.

Most of what divides people rests on unanswered questions about what is real: what a person is, what happens at death, what a life is for. Answer those questions together, and much of the division has nothing left to stand on.

If millions of people shared one holistic model of reality, then whether it proved right or wrong in its details, they would share a language, a purpose and a practice of caring for their bodies. A crew that agrees on where it is going can row together. A community like that would be collaborative, productive and, very likely, healthy even by today's scientific standards.

And if the HMR is right, the whole world changes.
