What Universe Model is.

Universe Model presents the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR): a narrative for humanity that explains reality as a whole, and a program to model it in working, physics-based simulations.

1. The problem

The problems humanity faces share a root.

Chronic disease is rising and is rarely reversed. Mental illness is widespread and poorly understood. People are divided over what is true, what is right and what life is for, between neighbors as much as between nations.

These look like separate problems, handled by separate experts: doctors, psychiatrists, scientists, priests, politicians. The HMR's view is that they share a root. Humanity has no agreed, complete picture of what reality is, what a human being is, and what a healthy body and mind are. Every field works from its own partial picture, and the pieces do not join up.

Without a shared picture of reality
  • Health has no defined healthy body to measure against.
  • Mental health has no agreed account of what is real to measure minds against.
  • Science describes how reality behaves, not what it is.
  • Values have nothing firm to rest on, so they divide us.
2. Models of reality

Everyone has a model of reality. Almost no one has a holistic one.

A model of reality is the picture of the world that any living thing acts on: what exists, how it behaves, what matters. Having one is not optional. A blade of grass, an animal and a human each operate on one, and each has higher or lower fidelity than another.

Humanity's best models are partial. Physics models matter, biology models life, medicine models disease, psychology models the mind, religion models meaning. Each can be divided into ever finer detail, and still miss what only appears when the system is seen as a whole.

A holistic model of reality is different in kind. It joins the parts into one connected account, so that understanding one part gives insight into every other.

Why holistic matters

When a system is understood as a whole, it can be modeled and changed with high fidelity. When even one important variable is unknown, that fidelity can fall to nearly zero, however precisely everything else is measured.

Picture an engineer who knows every part of a bridge except where it is anchored. The calculations can be perfect and the bridge still falls. Humanity's models of reality are in that position: excellent in their parts, with the anchor missing.

3. The Holistic Model of Reality

One account, from before the Big Bang to the human body.

The HMR proposes that physical reality emerges from Universal Intelligence (UI), a non-physical intelligence that has matured over time toward greater coherence, and that each human is an instance of UI living through a physical body. It draws on modern science and on the world's contemplative traditions, and is not affiliated with any religion.

Its most concrete and testable part is the human body understood as a tensegrity held together by fascia. That is where the work begins: with a new model of health, a model of mental health, and a physics-based simulation of the ideal human body.

4. Why it matters

The world a holistic model makes possible.

Health

Medicine with a defined healthy body to aim for. Conditions traced to their physical root and reversed where physics allows, with children growing toward the ideal instead of away from it.

Mind

Mental health measured against an agreed account of what is real and a defined healthy body, so that suffering is understood instead of feared.

Values

A shared context for a shared value system, from person to person, city to city and country to country, grounded in what reality is.

Science

A program that keeps everything science has established and gives its open questions a framework to be tested in.

Traditions

The insight of the world's body practices and contemplative traditions, finally readable in the language of physics.

Daily life

Everyday choices about the body, attention, relationships and purpose, made with a clear picture of what a human being is.

Today, one person holds the HMR. Imagine millions.

If millions of people shared one holistic model of reality, then whether it proved right or wrong in its details, they would share a language, a purpose and a practice of caring for their bodies. A community like that would be collaborative, productive and, very likely, healthy even by today's scientific standards.

And if the HMR is right, the whole world changes.
