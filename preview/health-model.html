HMR holistic health model

A model of health with fascia at the center: daily care that people do for themselves, specialists who look at the web first, diagnosis by movement and positional imaging, and diseases classified by the physics that produces them.

This is an entirely new healthcare paradigm. Under it, people across the world would live completely different daily lives, and their healthcare would look nothing like it does today.

Who does what

Most care moves home. The rest goes fascia-first

Everyone, every daySelf-sufficient

Every person knows how to care for their own fascia, as ordinarily as brushing their teeth, and does it daily from childhood. HMR Fascia Health Protocol →

The predictionAbout 90%

HMR predicts that if everyone practiced self-sufficient fascia care, about 90% of today's doctor visits would no longer be needed. It is a prediction, stated so that it can be tested, not a finding.

When more is neededFascia-first

The specialists who remain look at the web first: how its tension, density and gliding have changed, and what that change is pressing on, before reaching for a drug or a scalpel.

The whole person

Body, mind and meaning, together

Fascia care is the physical center of the model, not all of it. HMR's model of health also includes:

  • Meditation, as daily practice: attention trained on the body and on the mind itself.
  • Recognition of Universal Intelligence: knowing what a person is, in HMR's account, and where they fit. Universal Intelligence →
  • Values and purpose: a reason for the day, and a clear sense of how to treat others. HMR Values →

In HMR, a body that is cared for, a mind that is calm and a life that has a purpose are not three projects. They are one. Mental health today →

Why it starts with a reference

Medicine today learns what health is mostly from people who are unwell. HMR starts from the optimized human tensegrity, defined by physics, and measures every body against it. The Perfect Body →

In short

HMR's model of health puts the fascia web at the center. Most care moves home, as daily fascia care that people do for themselves and their families. Hospitals keep the emergencies and operations they do well, and specialists look at the fascia web first. Body and mind are treated as one system, because in HMR they are one.
