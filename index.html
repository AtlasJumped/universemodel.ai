A holistic model of the body, health and reality

Universe Model is the independent research program of Leo Emerson, home of the HMR. It studies the human body as a tensegrity held together by fascia, and asks how far that physical picture can explain health, development and experience.

Research focus

The work is organized as a working hypothesis, the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR), and tested against the scientific literature, including the evidence that limits or contradicts it.

The body as a tensegrity

Fascia as a continuous tensioned network: force transmission between muscles, prestress in tissues and cells, whole-body structural models.

Physics of health

Tissue sliding and adhesion, stiffness, posture and aging, and the mechanical side of conditions such as ankylosing spondylitis.

Body, sensing and mind

The nerves and sensors embedded in fascia, interoception, body maps in the brain, and what contemplative and movement traditions describe.

Coherence and emergence

Self-organization and synchronization in living systems, and how order arises in nature, read alongside philosophy of mind.

Approach

We gather open research from around the world into a private research library, read it with care, and record each finding with its source, separating what the evidence shows from what the hypothesis proposes. The aim is breadth and detail together: a model that agrees with established science where they overlap, and is honest about where it still needs work.

The program is non-commercial. AI tools assist with collecting and organizing the literature; the conclusions are checked against the original sources.
