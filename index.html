Our mission

To help build a holistic model of the body, health and reality, and to share it in a form other people can test, use and improve.

Universe Model is the independent research program of Leo Emerson and the home of the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR). The HMR starts from a working hypothesis: the body is best understood as a tensegrity held together by fascia, and seeing it that way explains health, development and experience more clearly than treating the body as separate parts.

We start with the body on purpose. Claims about mind and meaning earn a hearing when the physical work holds up first.

Fascia at two scales

We want to help advance what humanity knows about fascia in two places at once: in the measurable physical body, and in conscious experience.

In the physical body

Fascia is the collagen-rich connective tissue that forms continuous sheets and sleeves from head to foot. Measurement is starting to show what that continuity means.

  • Muscles pass some of their force sideways into neighbours and along fascial sheets, not only through their tendons.
  • Fascia can stiffen and soften, holds water, and lets layers slide over each other; when sliding fails, movement and comfort change.
  • Tensegrity models, from single cells to whole bodies, describe structures that stay stable under constant tension.

We collect the studies that support these ideas and the ones that limit them, side by side.

In conscious experience

Fascia is one of the body's largest sensory surfaces. It is dense with mechanoreceptors and free nerve endings that report stretch, pressure and strain.

  • Signals from this web feed interoception, the sense of the body's inner state, and the brain's maps of the body.
  • That raises open questions: how much of feeling at home in a body, or feeling tension, starts in fascia?
  • The HMR proposes that the fascial web is one place where intelligence and the physical body meet. That is a hypothesis, stated as one.

Here we are early. We say plainly what is measured and what is proposed.

How we work

We gather open research from around the world into one private research library: papers, books, datasets, anatomical images and video of skilled movers, in many languages. Each finding is recorded with its source and labelled as evidence or as hypothesis.

AI research assistants help collect, read and organise the literature. The conclusions are checked against the original sources, and evidence against the model is kept as carefully as evidence for it.

The program is independent and non-commercial.
