Independent research program

Health needs a reference point.

Most of what medicine knows about health was learned by studying illness and averaging across people who are not fully well. Universe Model is building the missing baseline: a detailed, physics-based model of the ideal human body. Measured against it, every symptom and disease becomes a deviation that can be traced, understood and, we hope, reversed.

The idea in one minute

Measure disease against health, not against other illness.

How health is defined today

  • Normal ranges come from population averages, and populations include many people who are not well.
  • Much of the evidence comes from studies and trials of people who are already sick.
  • Care starts where symptoms start, and often stops at managing them.
  • There is no agreed picture of what a fully healthy human body is.

The model we are building

  • Start from an ideal human body, male and female, built from real measurements with real physical limits.
  • Treat every symptom and disease as a measurable deviation from that baseline.
  • Trace the path from the ideal to each disease, to look for root causes rather than symptoms.
  • Use the same path, in reverse, to look for ways back toward health.
Why the body first

The body as a tensegrity.

Our working hypothesis is that the human body is best understood as a tensegrity: bones held in place by a continuous web of tensioned connective tissue called fascia, which wraps every muscle, organ, vessel and nerve without a break.

If that is right, then the shape of a body, how it moves, and where it stiffens, sticks or deposits fat are not random. They follow from physics. And physics can be modeled, measured and compared.

Our central hypothesis: make the fascia denser in one place, leave the rest ideal, and the physics alone will reshape the body elsewhere and strain the vessels, nerves and organs that run through it, in patterns that match real diseases.

Two ways in

A model of reality, and a model of health that stands on its own.

Universe Model is the home of the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR): the view that physical reality emerges from an underlying intelligent process, Universal Intelligence (UI), and that the human body is the vessel through which each of us experiences it.

You do not have to accept that view to use the second idea. A reference model of the healthy human body, and disease understood as measurable deviation from it, is a new paradigm for health on its own terms, open to testing by anyone.

"Humanity is using imperfect humans as the reference point for how to optimize humans. We need a gold standard."

Leo Emerson, founder. Everything here is a working hypothesis until the evidence says otherwise, and we keep the evidence against it as carefully as the evidence for it.
