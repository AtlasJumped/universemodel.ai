Humanity has no holistic model of reality

Physics describes matter, biology describes life, medicine describes illness, psychology describes the mind, and religion describes meaning. Each sees its own part clearly. None describes the whole, and the parts do not add up to one picture a person can live by.

We propose one: the Holistic Model of Reality (HMR).

“I am very astonished that the scientific picture of the real world around me is deficient. It gives a lot of factual information... but it is ghastly silent about all and sundry that is really near to our heart... It knows nothing of beautiful and ugly, good or bad, God and eternity.”

Erwin Schrödinger, physicist, a founder of quantum mechanics. Nature and the Greeks (1954). Source

The solution we propose

Three parts

HMR rests on three beliefs, one on each card: the mind is a Universal Intelligence, the body is connective tissue (often called fascia) working as a biotensegrity, and physics links the two. Pieces of each have been proposed before. In a search of the research literature we found only a handful of speculative preprints that touch all three, none of them peer reviewed and none built from the whole down to the part as HMR is. As far as we know, this site is the only place this picture is set out in full.

How HMR earns a hearing

A theory of everything you can test

People have heard of efforts to explain everything. Physics searches for a theory of everything that unites its forces. Neuroscience offers theories of consciousness. Philosophers propose that reality is mathematical, or that perception is an interface.

Each of these covers one piece: the forces, or the mind, or the mathematics. HMR aims at the whole, from the origin of physical reality to the human body, including consciousness, God, and health, and it comes with science and practical steps. It is in line with what many others have attempted, but it has no real comparator, because nothing else tries to connect all of these and then test the connection in a working model.

We start with HMR's most tangible part, and with the claims that science can check.

Compared with other theories of everything
  • String theory and loop quantum gravity: unite the forces of physics; say nothing about life, mind, or health.
  • Integrated information theory (Tononi, 2004) and the free energy principle (Friston, 2010): explain consciousness or the brain; not the origin of physical reality.
  • The mathematical universe (Tegmark, 2008) and the interface theory of perception (Hoffman, Singh and Prakash, 2015): bold accounts of what reality is; no model of the body.
  • Cosmological natural selection (Smolin, 1992): universes born inside black holes, with laws that evolve; leaves open where the first universe and its laws came from.
  • Vedanta, in Hindu philosophy: one consciousness, Brahman, appears as the world and as each self. The closest of all to HMR; what it lacks is the body as a connective tissue tensegrity.
  • HMR: one account from Universal Intelligence to the human body, tested first where it is most concrete.

How rigorous the simulation is

Evidence against the model is collected from the first day, alongside the evidence for it.
