HMR holistic health model

A model of health with connective tissue (often called fascia) at the center: daily care that people do for themselves, specialists who look at the web first, diagnosis by movement and positional imaging, and diseases classified by the physics that produces them.

This page is a brief snapshot of how all of health changes under HMR. The physical and the mental models follow in detail on the next pages.

This is an entirely new healthcare paradigm. Under it, people across the world would live completely different daily lives, and their healthcare would look nothing like it does today.

Who does what

Most care moves home. The rest goes connective-tissue-first

Everyone, every daySelf-sufficient

Every person knows how to care for their own connective tissue, as ordinarily as brushing their teeth, and does it daily from childhood, and teaches it to their children. HMR Connective Tissue Health Protocol →

The predictionAbout 90%

HMR predicts that if everyone practiced self-sufficient connective tissue care, about 90% of today's doctor visits would no longer be needed. It is a prediction, stated so that it can be tested, not a finding.

When more is neededConnective-tissue-first

The specialists who remain look at the web first: how its tension, density, and gliding have changed, and what that change is pressing on, before reaching for a drug or a scalpel.

The whole person

Body, mind, and meaning, together

Connective tissue care is the physical center of the model, not all of it. HMR's model of health also includes:

  • Meditation, as daily practice: attention trained on the body and on the mind itself.
  • Recognition of Universal Intelligence: knowing what a person is, in HMR's account, and where they fit. Universal Intelligence →
  • Values and purpose: a reason for the day, and a clear sense of how to treat others. HMR Values →

In HMR, a body that is cared for, a mind that is calm, and a life that has a purpose are not three projects. They are one. Mental health today →

Volume, up and down

HMR adjusts the tensegrity from the inside, from the outside, and through time. One way is to decrease and increase the body's volume on purpose, for short periods, with its own steps for each. To our knowledge, no health or fitness program other than bodybuilding prescribes eating more on purpose to increase volume. HMR holds that it belongs in a holistic protocol. This is HMR's hypothesis. HMR Connective Tissue Health Protocol →

Why it starts with a reference

Medicine today learns what health is mostly from people who are unwell. HMR starts from the optimized human biotensegrity, defined by physics, and measures every body against it. The Ideal Body →

Point by point

Today's model of care, and HMR's

Modern medicine sees the body as connected systems and works from symptoms. HMR sees one physical structure and works from the ideal.

AreaTodayIn HMR
Where it starts

A symptom, reported by someone who has never known full health. Tests look for the matching pattern.

The ideal body, defined by physics. Every body is measured against it, before and after symptoms appear.

How the body is seen

Connected systems of organs, each with its own specialty: heart, spine, gut, and mind are treated apart.

One physical structure, a biotensegrity, with connective tissue at the center. A change anywhere is felt everywhere.

Who gives the care

Mostly professionals, in short visits.

Mostly the person, every day at home, from the outside in and the inside out. Specialists take the rest, connective tissue first, with referrals and imaging.

How a problem is found

A described symptom, then tests and scans, most of them taken lying still.

Watching the body move, and imaging it standing, bending, stretched, on a full inhale, and on a full exhale.

When people are checked

When something goes wrong.

At critical points in life, such as puberty, and every one to two years, building a 3D profile of each person's biotensegrity that symptoms can be mapped against over a lifetime.

How disease is named

By its signs and the organ it shows up in.

By the physics that produces it: what the person was born with, outside agents, local adhesions from overuse and gravity, compression of what runs through the web, slow creep downstream, and errors in the body's signaling. HMR physical health model →

The mind

A separate field, with its own specialists.

Part of the same person: meditation, recognizing Universal Intelligence and one's role in it, and values and purpose. HMR mental health model →

The goal

Fix problems as they appear.

Prevent them, by keeping the web close to the ideal.

HMR's view

Why it would cost less

Positional imaging every year or two sounds expensive. HMR expects the opposite: when healthcare is refocused on connective tissue, much of what it pays for today shrinks, from repeat physical therapy to the facilities and tools that treat problems one at a time. The money moves from fixing problems to preventing them, and that makes these protocols inexpensive.

A prediction

Every person, a profile

Over a lifetime, each person builds a record of how their biotensegrity has changed. When a symptom appears, the question is not only what it is, but what changed in the web before it, and when. That is a dataset medicine has never had.

Three models, one person

The holistic model, in two halves

The body

HMR physical health model

Diagnosis by movement and positional imaging, the brain's two maps of the body, and a classification of disease by the physics that causes it.

 The mind

HMR mental health model

A map of a person's mental state built on what HMR says is true: their picture of reality, their body, and how they meet other people.
In short

HMR's model of health puts the connective tissue web at the center. Most care moves home, as daily connective tissue care that people do for themselves and their families. Hospitals keep the emergencies and operations they do well, and specialists look at the connective tissue web first. Body and mind are treated as one system, because in HMR they are one.
